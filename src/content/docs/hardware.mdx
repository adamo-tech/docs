Adamo encodes every camera with the robot's hardware H.264 encoder. This page
lists the computers we have measured, the encoder Adamo picks on each, and how
long a frame takes to get through the pipeline.

## Results

The numbers are the median **pipeline latency** in milliseconds: the time from
Adamo receiving a frame to the encoded H.264 frame being ready to send. It
includes copying the frame into the encoder and the encode itself. It does not
include camera capture, the network, or decoding on the operator's side.

<Aside type="tip" title="Getting the lowest latency">

- **Leave `encoder` unset.** Adamo picked the fastest working encoder on every
  machine above. The alternatives we tried were slower, dropped frames, or
  were not available.
- **Send NV12.** All results above use NV12 input, which the hardware encoders
  take directly. Other formats such as BGR are converted on the CPU first,
  which adds time on top of these numbers.
- **Prefer several 1080p cameras over one 4K camera.** Four 1080p streams
  carry the same pixels as one 4K stream, but each frame is ready sooner
  because it is a quarter of the size.

</Aside>

## How these were measured

- **Content.** A 2-second test clip of a camera panning across a cluttered,
  textured scene, with objects moving through it and one object rotating and
  growing close to the camera. It is generated from a fixed seed, so every
  machine encodes the same pixels. Encoders use their full bitrate on it, as
  they do with real camera footage.
- **Input.** The clip is loaded into memory and sent through `robot.video()`
  tracks as NV12 at the clip's frame rate, so reading frames costs nothing.
  4×1080p30 runs four tracks at once.
- **Bitrate.** Fixed, with adaptive bitrate off: 4 Mbps per 1080p30 stream,
  16 Mbps at 4K30 and 32 Mbps at 4K60.
- **Timing.** A 2-second warm-up is discarded, then 10 seconds are measured.
  The latency comes from the robot's own per-stage timings, which Adamo
  publishes once a second.
- **Software.** Adamo 1.0.27 in local mode, measured in November 2026.
